01 / THE PROBLEM

Making complex client relationships easier to understand.

Wealth advisors work across large amounts of client information. When important signals are distributed across different workflows and systems, identifying what requires attention can become difficult.

This project explored how a centralized intelligence layer could make relationship health, risk and opportunity more visible.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Turn fragmented client information into actionable intelligence.

11 / REFLECTION

Designing clarity inside complexity.

Designing for wealth management required balancing information density with clarity.

The central challenge was not simply presenting more information, but helping advisors understand what matters and why.

The project reinforced the importance of connecting research, information architecture and visual design into one continuous product-design process.
